FAQ

What documents can I create?

Service reports, maintenance and inspection checklists, test reports, work orders, job sheets and estimates - anything you regularly hand to customers. If you need something unusual, you can build your own template for any document.

Can I add a table to a report?

Yes. In the template editor you add a “Table” field and set the columns: text, number or a pick list (for example Pass / Fail). You add the rows on site, and the PDF shows a table whose header repeats on every page.

How do I send the PDF to my customer?

After generating it, tap “Send to customer” and pick text message, email or WhatsApp - the PDF goes as an attachment. On a computer, the file simply downloads.

How does RaportON know when to come back?

From the report. If you fill in the next service date, it shows up on your home screen under “Upcoming inspections”. From there, one tap starts a new report with the customer and equipment details, and if the report has a phone number you can text the customer a reminder.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes, the app needs a connection to sign in, save reports and sync your data. You can also install it on your phone like a regular app (it's a progressive web app).

Do I need a credit card?

No. The Free plan is free and doesn't ask for payment details. Sign up with email or a Google account and start creating reports right away.

Can I put my company logo on the reports?

Yes - in your company profile you upload your logo and add your details (address, phone, license number and anything else), and they appear at the top of every PDF automatically. Available on the Free plan too.

Will my reports meet code requirements?

RaportON helps you document your work clearly, but it doesn't make an inspection compliant by itself. Requirements differ by state, county and local authority (AHJ), and you're responsible for what goes into your reports. Our templates are a starting point you can adapt to the forms your jurisdiction requires.

Who owns my reports and data?

You do. We store your data only to run the service for you, we don't sell it, and we don't show ads. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
